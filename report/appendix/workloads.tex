\section{The workload sweep}
\label{app:workloads}

\file{08-workloads} tests H2 on workloads that put more of the bytes in small flows than our default of 90\% 10\,KB flows. It runs on the 256-router network of \file{03-load-sweep} at loads 0.3, 0.5 and 0.7, three seeds, with no bypass and with the shortest-path bypass, and compares each scenario with its own baseline on the same flows. \file{03-load-sweep} itself, at loads 0.1 to 0.9, is the default workload.

\paragraph{How much a cutoff moves.} For flow size $B$ and cutoff $T$, the share of flows counted as small is $\Pr(B \le T)$ and their share of bytes is $E[B \cdot \mathbf{1}(B \le T)] / E[B]$. \file{experiments/analysis/workload_cdf.py} computes both from a distribution, as the simulator samples it: linear interpolation between the distribution's points, rounded to whole bytes. Expected byte shares are 0.044\%, 1.02\% and 5.04\% for WebSearch at the three cutoffs, and 0.48\%, 7.91\% and 31.49\% for Hadoop. A cutoff changes which flows count as small, so modes should be compared within a scenario, not small-flow tails across cutoffs.

\paragraph{Running it.} The large distributions need far more memory per run than the default workload, up to an estimated 36\,GB for \texttt{fraction99} at load 0.7, so the sweep runs through its own queue, \file{experiments/run-workloads.sh}. It freezes the sources, programs and ns-3 libraries into the batch, plans its 210 runs from the experiment scripts, and starts each one only when its estimated memory fits a budget, backfilling with smaller runs. Runs that never fit stay pending and the batch ends as \texttt{blocked-memory}; at a 12\,GB budget these are the 24 \texttt{fraction99} runs and \texttt{fraction95} at load 0.7. As runs finish, \file{experiments/analysis/04-workloads.py} summarizes them.

\paragraph{Confidence intervals.} \file{experiments/analysis/bootstrap.py} gives paired bootstrap intervals for p99 FCT and the bypass-to-baseline ratio. It resamples whole topology seeds with both modes together, since flows within a run share queues and are not independent, and offers two estimands: the mean of each run's p99, or the p99 of the pooled flows. Unfinished flows count as infinitely slow, and ratio intervals are withheld when any resample is undefined. With three seeds there are only ten distinct resamples, so the intervals are exploratory: they cannot confirm a crossover even when they exclude a ratio of one.
